# Roadmap

What is planned after 1.2, and what is deliberately out of scope. Shipped work is in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md).

## Next: 1.3

- A plan library. Browse every question in the bank for your field and add any of them to the
  plan you are building. Save a plan as a named template (it suggests the job title and the
  date), then revise, rename or reuse your templates from their own page. A template can carry
  notes about the role, so when you ask the interviewer about the team or the job, it answers by
  quoting what you wrote, or says it does not know.
- Fixes from live interviews on 1.2.

## Planned

- Follow-up wording for each field. The fixed follow-up lines are the same for every field
  today.
- An IT support field, with its own vocabulary, round and case question. An IT role reads as a
  general interview with questions written from its description today.
- Questions written for the design and case rounds, once the measurements show how well each
  model writes them.
- Seniority labels for each field ("principal" rather than "staff" where that is the usual word).
- A larger evaluation: 150 to 250 labelled fixtures with a held-out part and a confusion matrix,
  a decision rule on the model's action probabilities, a small classifier that runs on the
  processor, merging the clarify action into its neighbours, and closing the known gaps on the
  junior plan.
- A provider for each task (the interview, the scoring, the planning), and a native Anthropic
  client.
- Code signing through SignPath's free programme for open-source projects, once the project
  qualifies. Until then Windows SmartScreen warns before the installer runs.
- A Microsoft Store package, the one free way to install without the SmartScreen warning.
- Measuring battery use during an interview on a laptop.

## Considered, not planned

- Cloud speech recognition. It would cost under US$0.20 an interview, but your recordings would
  leave your computer. Recordings stay local in every mode today.
- An IELTS speaking module.
- Hosting for several users. Mockingbird is a single-user app on your own computer.
- Regulated fields and sales role-play. Nursing, finance, law and sales need answer keys or
  role-play rather than behavioural scoring.
- A weighted overall score. Each criterion is reported on its own.
- Quoting the job description during the interview. It is shown on the plan page, in the
  history page and in the exports instead.
